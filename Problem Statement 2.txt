Problem Statement 2 - Building a Unified Student Engagement Ecosystem for Education and Financing Choices

1. Problem Statement
Indian students aspiring to pursue Graduate/postgraduate education face a fragmented journey—ranging from university discovery, application processes, visa requirements (if abroad),financial planning, and ultimately education financing.

Your challenge is to design and build an AI-first engagement platform that attracts,
educates, and nurtures students planning higher studies (both international and domestic)— ultimately building awareness of financing options and application for education loans.

The platform should:
• Act as a student ecosystem (e.g. SoFi for students)
• Use AI-led marketing, engagement, personalization, and automation
• Drive awareness → engagement → trust → conversion

2. Detailed Description
Objective
Create a prototype AI-powered platform (web/app/chatbot) that engages Indian
students across their study-abroad journey and seamlessly integrates education loan
discovery and application.
Target User Persona
• Second - Final-year undergraduates / young professionals (India-based)
• Planning to pursue postgraduate programs (abroad like US, UK, Canada, Europe,
etc.) or domestic institute
ions
• Early-stage (exploration) to mid-stage (applications in progress)

Core Requirements
A. AI-Driven Engagement Layer (Top of Funnel)
Design interactive engagement modules that keep students coming back:

Examples (you can innovate beyond these):
• AI Career Navigator: Suggests best-fit countries, universities, and courses
• ROI Calculator: Predicts salary outcomes vs cost of education
• Admission Probability Predictor
• Application Timeline Generator
• Conversational Mentor / Copilot (chatbot for doubts)

Must leverage:
• LLMs (for conversational guidance)
• Predictive models (for recommendations/scores)
• Generative AI (content, simulations, essays, etc.)

B. AI-Led Marketing & Growth Engine
Design how the platform will:
• Acquire users at scale using AI
• Personalize engagement journeys

You may Include (but not limited to):
• AI-generated content (blogs, reels, newsletters)
• Personalization engines (based on user behavior)
• Engagement loops (gamification, streaks, rewards, referrals)
• Smart nudges (timely notifications based on journey stage)
Think:
• “How does this platform become sticky and viral?”

C. Conversion Layer (Loan Funnel Integration)
Seamlessly integrate loan awareness and applications:
• Personalized loan eligibility estimator
• Dynamic loan offers (based on profile strength)
• Financial planning tools (EMI, repayment scenarios)
• AI-assisted loan application flow
• Document checklist + auto-fill support

D. AI-First Platform Design
The solution must be:
• Built largely using AI tools (no-code/low-code + APIs encouraged)
• Include:
o Frontend prototype (web/app)
o Backend logic (can be simulated)
o AI integrations (LLMs, APIs, agents)
Examples:
• Chatbot using LLM APIs
• Recommendation engine using simple ML logic
• Workflow automation using AI agents

Technical Expectations
Teams are encouraged to use:
• LLM APIs (OpenAI, Claude, etc.)
• No-code tools (Bubble, Webflow, Glide, etc.)
• Vector databases / embeddings (optional)
• Simple ML models or rule engines
• APIs (mock or real)
