## SCENE 4 — Multi-Step Onboarding (60–75s)

**Show:** 6-step KYC onboarding: Basic Info → Academic Background → Study Goals → Test & Readiness → Financial Details → Preferences.

**Voiceover (pace across steps):**

**Step 1 — Basic Info:**
"The student provides their name, phone, and city. This powers location-aware recommendations later."

**Step 2 — Academic Background:**
"Education level, field of study, institution, and GPA — these feed directly into our AI eligibility models for both university admission and loan qualification."

**Step 3 — Study Goals:**
"Target countries, preferred course, and intake timing. This is the foundation for personalised university matching and application timeline generation."

**Step 4 — Test & Readiness:**
"English language test status and scores. The platform adapts guidance based on whether the student has taken IELTS/TOEFL or still needs to."

**Step 5 — Financial Details:**
"Annual budget range and family income. This data directly powers our loan eligibility engine and helps match students to the right financing options."

**Step 6 — Preferences:**
"Communication preferences and study mode. Used for personalised engagement — AI knows when and how to reach the student."

**Action:** Fill each step → click Next → Submit at the end.

## RECORDING TIPS

- **Resolution:** 1920×1080 minimum
- **Browser:** Chrome, dark mode ON (looks cleaner on video)
- **Speed:** Don't rush onboarding — show each step clearly
- **Pause:** Let animations complete before moving (readiness ring, charts)
- **Loan page:** Spend the most time here — it's the conversion layer judges care about
- **Voice:** Confident, concise — avoid "um" and filler words
- **Music:** Subtle background track, keep volume low
