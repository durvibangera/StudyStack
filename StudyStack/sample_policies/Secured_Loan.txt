Lender: Poonawala Fincorp
Product: Secured Education Loan

Eligibility:
- Target Countries: USA, UK, Canada, Australia, Germany, Ireland, Europe, New Zealand
- Minimum GPA: 6.0 out of 10 or equivalent (Holistic academic evaluation due to collateral security)
- Supported Degrees: Masters (MS, MBA), STEM programs, Bachelors, Diploma
- Co-applicant: Mandatory. Minimum co-applicant income is INR 4,00,000 per annum (Evaluated leniently due to asset backing).

Financial Terms:
- Interest Rate: 9.25% to 10.5% (Lowest interest rate category)
- Max Loan Amount: Up to INR 15,000,000 (Very high limit, capped by the asset value)
- Processing Fee: 0.75% to 1.0% of loan amount
- Collateral Required: Fully secured. Requires a high-value asset covering 100%+ of the loan value.
  * Immovable Property: House, apartment, or non-agricultural land plots.
  * Liquid Assets: Fixed Deposits (FDs), government bonds, or cash-value life insurance policies.

Repayment:
- Moratorium Period: Course duration + 12 months
- Max Tenure: 180 months (15 years)
- Prepayment Penalty: Nil

Required Documents:
- Applicant KYC (Aadhar, PAN)
- University Admission Letter
- Co-applicant Income Proof
- Full Property Documents, title deed, tax receipts, or financial asset certificates for evaluation

Special Features:
- Near-zero risk for bank yields the lowest interest rates and largest loan amounts
- Longest repayment windows and most lenient academic evaluations
- Slower processing time due to property legal check and asset valuation
