Lender: Poonawala Fincorp
Product: Partially Secured Education Loan

Eligibility:
- Target Countries: USA, UK, Canada, Australia, Germany, Ireland, Europe
- Minimum GPA: 6.5 out of 10 or equivalent
- Supported Degrees: Masters (MS, MBA), STEM programs, Bachelors
- Co-applicant: Mandatory. Minimum co-applicant income is INR 6,00,000 per annum.

Financial Terms:
- Interest Rate: 10.75% to 11.75% (Moderate interest rate category)
- Max Loan Amount: Up to INR 7,500,000 (Higher limit than unsecured)
- Processing Fee: 1.0% to 1.5% of loan amount
- Collateral Required: Partially secured. Requires a low-value asset (e.g. Fixed Deposit, Gold, or property) that covers only part of the loan value. The remaining part is treated as unsecured risk.

Repayment:
- Moratorium Period: Course duration + 6 months
- Max Tenure: 144 months (12 years)
- Prepayment Penalty: Nil

Required Documents:
- Applicant KYC (Aadhar, PAN)
- University Admission Letter
- Co-applicant Income Proof (ITR, Salary Slips)
- Partial Collateral Proof (Fixed Deposit receipt, gold certificate, or partial property deeds)

Special Features:
- Hybrid model that lowers interest rates compared to fully unsecured loans
- Moderate processing time (usually 5-7 days)
- Allows families with smaller assets to unlock higher loan amounts
