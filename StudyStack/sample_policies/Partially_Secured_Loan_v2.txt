Lender: Poonawala Fincorp
Product: Partially Secured Education Loan

Eligibility:
- Target Countries: USA, UK, Canada, Australia, New Zealand, Europe
- Minimum GPA: 6.5 out of 10 or equivalent
- Supported Degrees: Undergraduate, Masters, PG Diplomas
- Co-applicant: Mandatory.

Financial Terms:
- Interest Rate: 9.75% to 11.25% (UPDATED: Reduced rate for partial collateral)
- Max Loan Amount: Up to INR 8,000,000 (UPDATED: Higher ceiling)
- Processing Fee: 0.75% to 1.25% of loan amount
- Collateral Required: Yes, but only partial. Value of collateral (property/FD) must cover at least 40% of the loan amount. (UPDATED: Lower collateral coverage required)

Repayment:
- Moratorium Period: Course duration + 6 months
- Max Tenure: 144 months (12 years)
- Prepayment Penalty: Nil

Required Documents:
- Applicant KYC (Aadhar, PAN)
- University Admission Letter
- Co-applicant Income Proof (ITR for last 2 years, Salary Slips)
- Property Documents or FD Receipts for the partial collateral portion

Special Features:
- Ideal for students needing higher amounts but lacking full collateral
- Better interest rates than purely unsecured loans
- Flexible collateral types accepted (residential, commercial, liquid assets)
